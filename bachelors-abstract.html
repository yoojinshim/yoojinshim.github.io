Evaluating GenAI Text Quality in Professional Writing

Bachelor's Thesis · Peking University, School of Journalism and Communication · 2024 · 95/100

Abstract

This study aims to explore the content characteristics and structural encoding of obituary reports in People's Daily, and attempts to reproduce this reporting pattern using generative artificial intelligence (AI) technology. Through in-depth analysis of 620 obituary reports published in People's Daily from 2016 to 2023, this paper reveals the standardized features of obituary reports, with the main move structure including notification, condolences, biography introduction, political positions, and honors, and develops reusable obituary templates based on these features. Utilizing the intelligent conversational agent Kimi Chat, this study further trains an AI model to generate obituary reports in the style of People's Daily based on the personal information of the deceased. Results from similarity analysis using the TF-IDF method indicate a high degree of similarity between AI-generated obituary reports and actual reports, particularly in obituaries of party members (excluding military personnel) and military personnel, demonstrating good replication capability by AI. This research provides theoretical and practical support for the intelligent production of news reports.

Keywords

Obituary, People's Daily, generative AI, move analysis, similarity analysis

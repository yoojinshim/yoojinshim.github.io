Segmented & Individualized Discount Optimization — Master's Thesis (RSM Erasmus)

Built a causal machine learning pipeline to personalize discount targeting for a European sportswear retailer's email marketing, balancing profit uplift against discount cost.

Python Causal Forest (IPW-adjusted) K-means (k=4) Off-Policy Evaluation Bootstrap

  • +1.22 margin uplift per customer under the dual-threshold targeting rule

Do LLMs Vary Parenting Advice by Gender?

Solo bias audit testing whether GPT-4 and Gemini 2.5 respond differently in tone to parenting questions based on the user's stated gender.

Python OLS Regression Prompt Auditing
  • GPT-4 sentiment toward mothers was 1.03 pts lower than toward fathers on a 5-pt scale (p<0.001)
  • Gemini 2.5 showed no significant gender-based sentiment gap (p=0.648)
  • The bias appeared in tone, not in advice content — emotionality itself didn't differ significantly
